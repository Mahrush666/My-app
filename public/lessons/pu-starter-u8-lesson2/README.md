# Power up Starter · Unit 8 · Lesson 2

Pictures extracted from the teacher-supplied U8 We are having fun Lesson 2.pptx: slides 3–7, images 11–15. Full pictures preserved as small WebP files. Vocabulary: eat, clean, drink, sleep, take a photo.

Five first-person action sentences and ten he/she action questions are bundled as English MP3 recordings (Jenny voice, slower pace). Playback uses local lesson-audio files served with the app. No live TTS or speech recognition request is needed during student practice.

Defaults, ages 3–5: Look & listen, Picture → choose the sound, Listen → choose the picture, Yes or no. Listening exploration is ungraded. The three question formats share one spaced review schedule per sentence. First incorrect attempts remain scheduled for another review even after an immediate correction. Students can deliberately practise all cards again after due reviews are complete.

Embedded PPT listening/colouring and Cameron tracks, video, colouring, writing and speech recognition are not included in this lesson release.
